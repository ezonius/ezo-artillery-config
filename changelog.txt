Version: 1.0.1
Date: 2026-10-20
  Bugfixes:
    - The shell damage and splash radius multipliers were never applied to vanilla artillery shells. The projectile
      reference lookup only checked data.raw["projectile"], but artillery shells use the dedicated prototype type
      "artillery-projectile" (data.raw["artillery-projectile"]) and were silently skipped. The walk now checks both
      prototype types, follows ammo items matching the gun's ammo_category (vanilla artillery guns define no
      ammo_type of their own), recurses into nested-result/area actions, and scales area action radii.
